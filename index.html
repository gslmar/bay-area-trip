Before you go

Checklist

    Ticks are saved on this device only.

    Getting between cities

    High-speed rail tickets are tied to each passport. Book on the 12306 app as soon as sales open (about 15 days ahead), or at the West Kowloon counter, and bring everyone's passport. Times on the day pages are targets: take the nearest train.

    Money

    • Hong Kong: HKD. Load an Octopus card per person for MTR, buses and 7-Eleven.
    • Mainland: RMB. Almost everything is paid with Alipay or WeChat Pay. Link a card in both apps before you leave.
    • Carry a little RMB cash for older relatives and small stalls.

    Phones

    • Instagram, WhatsApp and Google don't work on mainland Wi-Fi. Roaming or a travel eSIM usually still reaches them.
    • Use Amap (高德地图) for maps and Didi for taxis on the mainland.

    Entry and plugs

    • Check that every passport has 6+ months left, and check current entry rules before you fly.
    • Hong Kong uses UK three-pin plugs. Mainland sockets take flat two-pin (Type A) or angled three-pin (Type I) plugs. Pack a universal adapter.
